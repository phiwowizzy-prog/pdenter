The Flesch–Kincaid Formula, Explained

The Flesch–Kincaid readability tests are widely used to estimate how difficult a text is to read. LexiCount Pro's Readability Checker displays two common scores:

  • Flesch Reading Ease — a score from 0 to 100 where higher is easier to read. Short sentences and common words increase the score.
  • Flesch–Kincaid Grade Level — estimates the U.S. school grade required to understand the text.

Formulas are shown in the tool's details so you can verify the calculation — transparency builds trust. Use these scores to tailor content to your audience: lower grade levels for broad consumer content, and higher levels for technical or academic material.
