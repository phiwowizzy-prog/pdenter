How Many Words Is a 5-Minute Speech?

Use our speaking-time calculator to estimate word counts for presentations and talks.

When preparing a short speech, timing matters. A typical speaking rate for a clear, practiced presentation is around 120 to 160 words per minute. That means a 5‑minute speech usually contains between 600 and 800 words. If you read quickly or include fewer pauses, you may reach 900‒1000 words; for a slower, more deliberate delivery, aim for 500–600 words.

Tips for a strong 5-minute speech

  • Start with a short hook and a clear thesis.
  • Limit to 2–3 main points to avoid rushing.
  • Practice with aloud timing to match pace and pauses.
