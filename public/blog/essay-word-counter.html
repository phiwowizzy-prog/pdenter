Essay Word Counter: How to Meet Requirements

Students often need to hit an exact word count. LexiCount Pro shows as-you-type word and character counts so you can meet assignment requirements without surprises. Remember to check whether footnotes, references, or titles are counted by your instructor.

Estimating pages

Use the Word-to-Page estimator on LexiCount Pro to convert word counts into printed pages based on font and size. As a rule of thumb, 250–300 words per page is a common estimate for double-spaced 12pt text.
