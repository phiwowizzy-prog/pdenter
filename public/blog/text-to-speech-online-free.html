Text to Speech Online — Free Options and Tips

Text-to-speech is a powerful tool for proofreading, accessibility, and content review. LexiCount Pro provides a free, browser-based TTS that uses your device's built-in voices. Because TTS runs locally, your text remains private.

How to use TTS effectively

  • Adjust the speaking rate to match natural pacing.
  • Listen for awkward phrasing and long sentences — they stand out when read aloud.
  • Use TTS to proof emails, essays, or long-form content to catch issues you missed while reading.
