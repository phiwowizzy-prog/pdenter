Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-06

LexiCount Pro™ is committed to protecting your privacy. We designed our core tools to process text in your browser — by default your text is not sent to our servers. This reduces risk and preserves privacy.

Information We May Collect

For core, client-side tools we collect no text data. For features that require server processing (OCR, AI summarization, premium voice processing) we transmit only the data you choose to process; such files are deleted automatically within 24 hours unless you opt in to a saved history (premium feature).

Cookies & Advertising

We use a cookie consent banner to comply with EU/EEA/UK regulations. If you enable advertising features or sign up for analytics, we may load third-party scripts (AdSense, analytics). We do not sell personal data.

Security

We use HTTPS/SSL for all connections. For server-side features we follow reasonable industry practices to secure data in transit and at rest.
